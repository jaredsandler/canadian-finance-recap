# Canadian Finance — April 2026 Recap

An interactive dashboard summarizing notable stories in Canadian finance and business for April 2026.

Built from the [weekly newsletter by Jared Sandler](https://assetequation.substack.com), which covers Canadian finance in a digestible weekly format — fintech, the big banks, pensions, capital markets, sponsorships, and more.

## What's inside

- **Company mention frequency** — see which companies got the most coverage, with expandable context on where and why each one appeared
- **Browse by post** — filter stories by topic (Fintech, Pensions, ETFs, Capital Markets, etc.) and click into any of the 5 posts covered
- **Direct story links** — every story title links directly to that section of the original newsletter post
